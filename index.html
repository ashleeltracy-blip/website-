A neutral, experienced fact-finder

When a workplace complaint arises — harassment, discrimination, retaliation, misconduct, or a violation of policy — organizations need someone outside the chain of command to look at the facts objectively. As a solo practitioner, I serve as that independent, third-party investigator.

I gather evidence, interview witnesses, and apply the relevant legal standards, personnel policies, collective bargaining provisions, and just cause principles to reach well-reasoned, defensible conclusions — delivered in a clear written report your organization can act on with confidence.

How an Investigation Works

A structured process designed for fairness, thoroughness, and a clear paper trail.

  1. 1

    Intake & Scope

    Review the complaint, applicable policies, and collective bargaining language to define the scope and standard of review.

  2. 2

    Evidence Gathering

    Collect and review relevant documents, communications, personnel records, and other supporting evidence.

  3. 3

    Witness Interviews

    Conduct structured, trauma-informed interviews with the complainant, respondent, and witnesses.

  4. 4

    Analysis

    Weigh the evidence and credibility, and apply the applicable policy, contractual, and legal standards.

  5. 5

    Written Findings of Fact

    Deliver a clear, well-organized report with findings, credibility determinations, and conclusions.
